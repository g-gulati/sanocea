## Priority reading

**High-value, near-term:** Flipkart and Amazon India need only credential/consent steps (no further spec
work) — pursuing real seller/merchant onboarding for these two is the fastest path to a first
REAL_ACCOUNT_CERTIFIED marketplace connector. **High-value, spec-blocked:** Flipkart Minutes (smallest
remaining gap) and Blinkit (best-evidenced unimplemented channel) are the two quick-commerce candidates
worth an actual outbound request. **Documentation-blocked, worth a combined ask:** Meesho and Myntra
both need real technical material more than they need credentials. **Not yet worth an outbound request:**
Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Amazon Now, BigBasket/BB Now — evidence is too thin to know what to even ask
for; a request today would be speculative.
