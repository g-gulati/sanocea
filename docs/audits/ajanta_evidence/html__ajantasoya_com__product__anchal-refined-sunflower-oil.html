Why Anchal Refined Sunflower Oil is the Perfect Choice for Indian Cooking

Indian cooking requires oil that performs consistently across multiple cooking styles.
Anchal Refined Sunflower Oil has a neutral taste, ensuring the natural flavours of spices and ingredients remain unchanged.
Its high smoke point makes it suitable for frying, sautéing, tempering, and deep-frying. The oil is light and non-greasy, helping food feel lighter even when cooked daily.

Know More About Soyabean Oil

We have listed the most frequently asked questions about our oil

What is Anchal sunflower oil, and how is it made?

Anchal sunflower oil is a type of vegetable oil extracted from sunflower seeds. It's produced through a process of pressing the seeds to extract the oil or by solvent extraction. The oil is then refined to remove impurities and improve its shelf life.

What are the health benefits of using Anchal Sunflower Oil?

Sunflower oil is rich in Vitamin E, low in saturated fats, and has a high content of unsaturated fats, including omega-6 fatty acids. It may help in maintaining heart health, managing cholesterol levels, and supporting overall well-being.

Can I use Anchal sunflower oil for cooking at high temperatures?

Yes, refined sunflower oil and high-oleic sunflower oil are suitable for high-heat cooking methods like frying, baking, and roasting due to their high smoke points.

Is sunflower oil a good option for individuals with allergies?

Sunflower oil is generally considered safe for individuals with common food allergies, such as nuts and soy. However, if you have specific allergies or concerns, it's best to consult with a healthcare professional.

Is sunflower oil suitable for a vegan diet?

Yes, sunflower oil is plant-based and entirely vegan-friendly.

How should I store sunflower oil, and what is its shelf life?

Store sunflower oil in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight and heat. The shelf life of properly stored sunflower oil is usually around 1 to 2 years

Can sunflower oil be used for skincare and hair care?

Yes, sunflower oil is often used in skincare products and hair treatments due to its moisturizing and nourishing properties.

Is sunflower oil good for deep frying?

Yes, sunflower oil's high smoke point makes it an excellent choice for deep frying, as it can withstand high temperatures without breaking down and compromising the food's taste and texture.
